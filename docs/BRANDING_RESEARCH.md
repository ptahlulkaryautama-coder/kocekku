# Sakku — Branding Research & Name Suggestions

---

## 1. Current State

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Current Name | Kocekku |
| Origin | Indonesian — "kocek" (pocket) + "ku" (my) = "My Pocket" |
| Product Type | Personal / Family Finance PWA |
| Target Market | Expanding from Indonesia to international/English-speaking |
| Design Direction | Premium fintech · Calm · Trustworthy · Analytical · Approachable |
| Tagline (current) | "Your Money. One Clear Picture." |

**The Challenge:** "Kocekku" works brilliantly in Indonesia but has zero recognition internationally. It's hard to pronounce for English speakers, hard to spell, and doesn't communicate the product's purpose. A rebrand is needed that either (a) keeps Indonesian roots as a unique differentiator or (b) starts fresh with a globally resonant name.

## 2. Fintech Naming Landscape (2025–2026)

### What Top Apps Are Called

| Category | Examples | Pattern |
|----------|----------|---------|
| **Metaphor** | Acorns, Robinhood, Rocket Money | Evokes growth, freedom, momentum |
| **Emotive** | Chime, Monarch, SumUp, Comfort | Feeling-first, approachable |
| **Descriptive** | Credit Karma, Personal Capital, TurboTax | Tells you what it does |
| **Compound** | LendingTree, SmartAsset, MoneyLion | Two words combined |
| **Coined/Abstract** | Klarna, Nubank, Revolut | Invented words, highly ownable |
| **Positioning** | Empower, Pinnacle, Premier | Claims leadership |
| **Anthemic** | Ally, Affirm, Rally | Call-to-action, declarative |
| **Disruptive** | Lemonade, Fabric, Petal | Unexpected words in fintech |

### Key Trends

1. **One-word names dominate** — Monarch, Chime, Klarna, Revolut, Digit, Stripe, Ally, Trim, Rally
2. **Fintech names skew metaphorical**, while traditional banks use descriptive names
3. **4–7 characters** is the sweet spot for memorability
4. **Soft consonants** (M, N, L, S) feel trustworthy; **hard consonants** (K, T, P) feel bold/techy
5. **Nature metaphors** are overused in wealth management (Bay, Peak, Stone, Green) — a personal finance app can differentiate by avoiding them
6. **Indonesian/Asian roots** are a genuine differentiator in global fintech — GoPay, OVO, DANA, Dana proved short Asian names can work globally

## 3. Naming Strategy Options

### Option A: Keep "Kocekku" with a Global Sub-brand
**Strategy:** Preserve brand equity while making it accessible
**Example:** Kocekku — Your Money, One Clear Picture
**Pros:** Retains Indonesian identity; existing users keep their brand
**Cons:** Hard to pronounce internationally; no immediate product understanding

### Option B: New Global Name
**Strategy:** Fresh start with worldwide appeal
**Pros:** Clean slate; can be purpose-built for global market
**Cons:** Loses existing brand recognition in Indonesia

### Option C: Evolve the Indonesian Meaning
**Strategy:** Use the "pocket" concept but make it universal
**Example:** Pocketline, Pocketwise, Pocketa
**Pros:** Bridges Indonesian roots to English
**Cons:** "Pocket" is common in finance; differentiation is harder

### Option D: New Metaphor / Abstract Name
**Strategy:** Create something ownable and distinctive
**Pros:** Maximum differentiation; highly trademarkable
**Cons:** Requires brand building from zero meaning

## 7. Recommendations

### Primary Recommendation: **Nestiq**

**Why Nestiq wins:**

1. **Family finance positioning** — "Nest" immediately evokes home, family, nest egg. This directly matches Kocekku's family finance DNA
2. **"IQ" suffix** — Communicates intelligence, analytics, financial health. The app literally has a Financial Health scoring system
3. **Unique** — No major fintech company currently uses this name
4. **Trademarkable** — Coined word, strong trademark potential
5. **Global** — Easy to pronounce in English, Spanish, French, German, and Asian languages
6. **Visual branding potential** — Nest imagery (circle, layers, protection) works beautifully for a finance app icon
7. **Scalable** — Doesn't box you into "budgeting" or "tracking" — it can expand to insurance, investments, family planning
8. **Domain** — nestiq.com, getnestiq.com likely available (should verify)

### Secondary Recommendation: **Kocek**

**Why Kocek is worth considering:**

1. **Retains Indonesian roots** — Shows where the app came from
2. **Natural English pronunciation** — "Koh-check" sounds like "checking" money
3. **Short** — 5 characters, highly memorable
4. **Tagline synergy** — "Check your money. Every day." is perfect
5. **Cultural bridge** — Indonesian users keep their brand; international users get an easy, fintech-sounding name
6. **Domain** — kocek.app, getkocek.com likely available

### Honorable Mention: **Savia**

For a premium, sophisticated feel with no cultural baggage. Best if the app aims for a wealth-management tone.

## 8. What to Avoid

| Pattern | Why | Examples |
|---------|-----|----------|
| Generic "money" names | Overcrowded, hard to trademark | MoneyTracker, CashManager |
| Overly technical | Alienates casual users | CryptoLedger, Blockchain Wallet |
| Names with negative connotations | "Wallet" implies limited spending power | PoorWallet, DebtTracker |
| Names too similar to competitors | Confusion risk | Mintly (≈ Mint), Koinly (≈ crypto tool) |
| Long compound names | Hard to remember, type, say | FinancialClarityPro |
| All-caps styling | Feels corporate, not fintech | MONEYPLUS, FINTRACK |
